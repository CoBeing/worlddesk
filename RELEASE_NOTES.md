# WorldDesk v0.1.0-preview.2 — macOS first

A bilingual, China-friendly preview. Download the unsigned macOS Apple Silicon ZIP.

What changed:
- Chinese / English UI: follows operating system language, manual switch persists independently of location.
- Approximate IP location via GeoJS with fallback; manual list of Chinese/global cities.
- China-first station catalog and locally computed sunrise, sunset and moon phase.
- Nearby observations (roughly 2,000 km) and bundled Natural Earth coastline map; no outside tile CDN.
- Removed external CoBeing / God’s Eye View navigation from the app.
- Bundled Local Bridge 0.1.0 sidecar; no Provider permissions or background daemon.
- Local favorites, real observations and radio remain available.

Limits:
- Unsigned and unnotarized: verify checksum and trust source.
- IP location may be wrong for VPN/mobile networks; change city anytime.
- Third-party stations, map data and event feeds may be incomplete, stale or unavailable; raw source titles are not automatically translated.
- No official CENC feed, licensed weather API, Agent run, SDR or messaging, embedded God’s Eye View 3D or CoBeing account sync.
- Windows and Linux deliberately not published from this macOS-first tag.
